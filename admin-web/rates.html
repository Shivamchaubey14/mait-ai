A rate left at zero is not free

It reads as unpriced, and the app tells the Mait to ask an administrator rather than quoting a figure the dairy cannot stand behind. That holds for the pregnancy rate as much as for the breeds. Every figure here reaches the handsets on their next request, and applies from then on — a visit already recorded keeps the price it was charged at, so re-pricing today never restates last month.

A refused check is never charged. Nothing was examined, so there is nothing to collect and nothing to deduct.
