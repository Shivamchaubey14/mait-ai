Zones

—

A zone is a group of BMC/MCCs, and it decides what an account sees.

The chilling centres come from the SAP master data — they cannot be typed in here. Each one belongs to a single zone, so adding it to a second means taking it out of the first. Give an account its zone on Users & roles; an account with no zone sees the whole network.

Who runs these zones

—

A zone and a mobile number together make the app open.

A zonal manager works away from a desk, so they sign in on a handset with a code sent to this number — the zone is what they see there, and it is the one above. Neither alone is enough: no number means no code, and an account with no zone stays a portal login. Which screens they open is still Users & roles.
