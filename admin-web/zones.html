Zones

—

A zone is a group of BMC/MCCs, and it decides what an account sees.

The chilling centres come from the SAP master data — they cannot be typed in here. Each one belongs to a single zone, so adding it to a second means taking it out of the first. Give an account its zone on Users & roles; an account with no zone sees the whole network.
